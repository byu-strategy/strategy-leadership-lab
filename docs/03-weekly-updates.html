Weekly Updates

Every student in the Lab sends a written end-of-week update to the person they report to, with strategy-program@byu.edu in CC.

That is the whole mechanism, and it is accountability all the way up the chain. A club VP reports to their co-presidents. Those co-presidents report to Soraya. The program co-presidents report to Scott. Nobody reports into a void, and nobody is left guessing what the people below them did this week.

The CC does two things: it gives the Strategy Program one place to track completion, and it means your leader is never the only person who knows you did the work.

If you hold two roles

Some leaders serve in two places at once, for example a program VP who is also a club VP. Your choice how to handle it:

  • One email addressed to both leaders, with the body split by role so each of them can find their part, or
  • Two separate emails, one to each leader, each covering only that role.

Either way it counts as one update for the week. CC strategy-program@byu.edu on whatever you send.

If you receive updates from people below you, read them before you write your own. Your update should reflect what your team did, not only what you personally touched.

Deadline

Thursday by 11:59 PM

Thursday rather than the weekend, so every update is in hand before Friday’s session. What you write becomes the Announcements block: if there is an event, a deadline, or a problem the room should know about, the update is how it gets there.

  • First update due: Thu, Sep 10, ahead of session 2
  • Final update due: Thu, Dec 3, ahead of session 13
  • No update due Thu, Nov 26. Thanksgiving, and there is no class Nov 27.
  • Total updates: 12

Session 1 on Sep 4 is the kickoff, so there is no update before it. Every session after that has one.

Format

Three sections, the same ones used in the APM Lab internship. Keep it to one screen.

1. What I did this week [Required]

  • Completed events, meetings, outreach, and deliverables
  • Lead with outcomes, not activity. “42 students at the case workshop” beats “held a case workshop.”
  • Include numbers when you have them: attendance, applications, offers, signups

2. What I am doing next week [Required]

  • Priorities and planned work, with dates
  • Be realistic about what fits in a week

3. Blockers, risks, or dependencies [Omit if none]

  • Anything that might slow you down
  • What you need from your leader or another team
  • Decisions you are waiting on

One extra section for club leaders

Club co-presidents add a fourth section, because their updates are what the Friday Announcements block is built from.

4. What students should know [Required for club leaders]

  • Upcoming events with dates, deadlines, and how to participate
  • Written for a student who is not in your club and may not be a strategy major. Spell out acronyms and say what the event is for.
  • Write it so it can be read aloud in class on Friday without editing.

Example

Subject: PMA Weekly Update - [Name] - Week of Sep 8

Hi Soraya,

What I did this week:
- Ran the PM resume workshop, 38 students attended, 22 from outside the strategy major
- Confirmed Adobe for the Oct 2 info session
- Sent 14 alumni outreach emails, 6 replies so far

Next week:
- Finalize the fall mentorship pairings (Maya owns this)
- Post the case competition signup form
- First mock interview round, Thursday evening

Blockers:
- Still need a room for Oct 2, the request has been pending two weeks
- Our marketing VP is out for a family emergency through the 20th

What students should know:
- PMA is hosting an Adobe info session Thu, Oct 2 at 5:00 PM in the Tanner Building.
  Open to any major. No signup required.
- Mentorship applications close Fri, Sep 19. Anyone interested in product management
  can apply, first or fourth year, any major.

Thanks,
[Name]

A VP update is shorter. Three sections, often five or six bullets total.

Grading

Twelve updates, graded individually. Points and category weight are set on the Assessments page.

Status Credit
On time, Thursday by 11:59 PM Full
Late, by Friday 12:30 PM (start of class) Half
After class starts None

The late cutoff is the start of class because an update that arrives after the session has already missed its second job, which is feeding the room.

Missing the CC means the update cannot be tracked, and an untracked update does not count. Add strategy-program@byu.edu to your contacts in week one.

What Makes a Good Update

  • Short. Bullets, not paragraphs. If it runs past one screen, cut it.
  • Specific. Numbers, names, dates. “Good turnout” tells your leader nothing.
  • Honest about blockers. Flagging a problem early is the entire point. A clean update every week from a team that is struggling is worse than no update at all.
  • Consistent. The value is in the streak. Twelve on-time updates build a record of the semester that nobody has to reconstruct in December.
